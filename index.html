Housing Retrofit Seminar

Can the most vulnerable neighborhoods in our cities benefit from urban growth occurring in other areas? This question guides research into an innovative instrument: Transfer of Development Rights (TDR).

The idea is simple but (we think) powerful: residential buildings in vulnerable neighborhoods that need rehabilitation could "sell" their growth potential (buildable square meters they won't use) to developers in other areas of the city where it makes sense to build taller or at higher density. The proceeds from those sales would be directed toward retrofitting the buildings that need it.

But then, which areas should be selected to receive/purchase DR? How do we define and identify their viability? This seminar is framed as a quantitative approach to these questions.

Students will learn hedonic regression techniques, Principal Component Analysis, clustering, and spatial econometrics—applied to real data of Barcelona. The final deliverable will be an evidence-based territorial prioritization proposal for public intervention.
